About BLRnews

Bengaluru's independent local news source

BLRnews is a Bengaluru-focused news website covering city affairs, traffic updates, politics, technology, and sports. Our mission is to bring fast, accurate, and hyperlocal news to residents of Bengaluru.

We believe in straightforward journalism — no sensationalism, no clickbait. Just the news that matters to people who live, work, and commute in this city.

What we cover

  • City infrastructure and civic issues
  • Traffic and transport updates
  • Local politics and governance
  • Bengaluru's tech and startup ecosystem
  • Sports, weather, and neighbourhood news
