Bengaluru Metro Phase 3 gets green signal — new corridors announced

👤 City Desk 📅 June 4, 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

The Bengaluru Metropolitan Region Development Authority confirmed on Thursday that Metro Phase 3 has received final central government approval, paving the way for work to begin on three new corridors spanning over 44 kilometres across the city.

The announcement comes after years of planning, feasibility studies, and environmental clearances. Officials say the corridors will connect Hebbal in the north, Sarjapur in the southeast, and Tumkur Road in the west — areas that have seen explosive growth but remain underserved by public transport.

"This will transform daily commutes for over 8 lakh residents who currently depend on private vehicles or overcrowded BMTC buses," said a senior BMRCL official at the press conference.

What the new corridors cover

The three corridors have been designed to ease congestion on the city's most traffic-heavy roads. Corridor 1 stretches from Hebbal to Nagawara, Corridor 2 runs from Sarjapur Road to Electronic City, and Corridor 3 connects Tumkur Road to Peenya Industrial Area.

Construction is expected to begin in early 2027 following the completion of land acquisition, with tunnelling work on the underground sections scheduled to start by mid-2027. The entire Phase 3 network is targeted for operational status by 2030.

What this means for commuters

Residents along the new corridors can expect significant changes to their daily commute once the metro lines are operational. The Sarjapur corridor, in particular, is expected to reduce travel time from the tech hub to MG Road from 90 minutes by road to under 30 minutes by metro.

BMRCL has also announced feeder bus services and last-mile connectivity infrastructure at all new stations, including dedicated cycling lanes and auto-rickshaw bays.
